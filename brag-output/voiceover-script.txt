This is Dancing with the SARs — Flood Pulse. A NASA Space Apps desk for reading Earth change through radar, without pretending every colorful overlay is a measurement.

Start in Atlas. Pick Japan or Brazil. Run a thirty-second flood or fire demo grounded in real NASA Earth Observatory references — Typhoon Hagibis over Honshu, Amazon fire season heat — while the terrain progression stays honestly synthetic.

Then open the three-D globe. Spin Blue Marble Earth. Hover a country for its name. Switch between two map modes: the synced Natural Earth coasts with Blue Marble land detail, or the full real Blue Marble photograph. Prefer a clean look? Flip on the white background behind the globe.

Need live evidence? Live Sources pulls ASF and Earthdata catalogs — latest windows, kept separate from teaching overlays so the signal stays honest.

Pakistan surface atlas and change signals sit beside compare and the radar classroom. Same rule everywhere: show the source, name the demo, never mix catalog truth with illustrative flood and fire sectors.
